## Entry qualification

Applicants must hold a **Bachelor degree or higher**.

Applicants must hold a first-cycle undergraduate degree from a Higher Education Institution in Greece or an equivalent recognized foreign institution, in accordance with Law 4957/2022 as in force.

Entry-qualification documents are accepted in **English or Greek**.

When original documents were issued in another language, applicants must provide certified official translations together with copies of the original documents.

Applicants are encouraged to ask whether their institution can issue transcripts or other required documents in English.

## Overview

### Why this MSc?

The MSc in Advanced Cybersecurity Technologies & Governance is interdisciplinary and cross-sectoral in nature and focuses on the specialisation of scientists in cybersecurity, data protection, and the governance of relevant environments.

The programme prepares students to explore complex cybersecurity challenges, develop innovative solutions, and contribute to digital resilience, risk management, and evidence-based policy.

The programme is delivered within the EU-iNSPIRE framework, a European initiative supporting education, skills development, and certification to address the growing cybersecurity skills gap in Europe.

### What students will learn

The page lists advanced knowledge in the following core areas:

- Data protection principles and regulatory frameworks.
- Compliance and governance.
- Risk assessment and impact evaluation.
- Privacy by design and privacy-enhancing technologies.
- Analysis of technologies and ethical issues.
- Cybersecurity and risk management.
- Network and IoT security.
- Digital well-being and human risk factors.
- Security testing and ethical penetration of systems.
- Security operations centers and incident management.
- Specialized security applications.
- Advanced protection technologies.
- Cyber policy and international relations.
- Cyber resilience and emerging threats.

## Programme structure

The curriculum follows a progressive structure from foundational cybersecurity concepts to advanced technical and governance applications.

The curriculum is aligned with European skills frameworks and industry standards in cybersecurity and digital resilience.

### First semester

Four obligatory courses:

- Privacy and Data Protection.
- Cybersecurity Governance.
- Network Security.
- IoT Security.

### Second semester

Two obligatory courses:

- Digital Wellbeing in Cyber-space.
- Offensive Security.

Students select two optional courses from the following list:

- Forensics.
- Secure Autonomous Systems.
- Cybersecurity: Attack, Defence, and Operational Practice.
- Cybersecurity in Industrial Scenarios.
- Cybersecurity in the Political Domain: Internal and External Dimensions.

### Third semester

- M.Sc. Thesis, obligatory.

## Career opportunities

Students will acquire knowledge and practical experience intended to enhance their career prospects and support entry into a wide range of employment sectors.

The programme develops highly specialised knowledge and skills that address increasing public- and private-sector needs in cybersecurity, data protection, and governance of related environments.
