# MSc in Advanced Cybersecurity Technologies and Governance

This is the canonical workspace for the distance-learning MSc offered by the Department of Digital Systems at the University of Piraeus.
It contains the application material, official admissions records, working decisions, private supporting documents, and future study documentation.

## Current status - 23 August 2026

- Expression of Interest: submitted.
- Formal programme approval: completed.
- Application window: open from 13 August through Monday, 21 September 2026.
- Application portal: [apply.ponts.academy/enter/131](https://apply.ponts.academy/enter/131).
- Application fee: EUR 200 and non-refundable.
- Application editing: progress can be saved before submission, but the application locks after submission.
- Interview: a mandatory asynchronous online video interview follows submission and has a separate deadline delivered by email.
- Tuition: the official page lists free tuition for EU citizens for academic years 2026-2027 and 2027-2028, and EUR 5,000 total for non-EU citizens.
- Programme start: courses begin in October.
- Programme structure: three academic semesters and 90 ECTS, with the third semester devoted to the MSc thesis.

The July informational email did not require a reply or submission by email.
The application must now be completed through the official portal.

## Immediate priorities

1. Create the portal account and inspect every required task, file format, and referee field.
2. Secure two strong referees and one backup.
3. Locate the degree certificate, official transcript, English-language evidence, and ID or passport copy.
4. Personalize and finalize the seven drafted statements.
5. Prepare and pay the EUR 200 application fee through the official portal.
6. Submit before 21 September, save all confirmations, and complete the separate video interview by its emailed deadline.
