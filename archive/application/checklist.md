## Referee strategy

Recommendation quality and specificity matter more than the academic or professional label.
The programme explicitly accepts faculty members and/or employers.

The preferred pairing is:

1. One strong current or recent manager who can discuss platform responsibility, technical leadership, collaboration, judgment, and delivery at scale.
2. The bachelor's thesis supervisor if they remember the work well and can provide a specific, enthusiastic assessment of academic ability, research, writing, and independent thinking.

If the thesis supervisor would provide only a vague letter, use two strong managers with complementary perspectives instead.
A referee does not need to be a cybersecurity specialist, but the letter should connect observed evidence to security-relevant qualities and readiness for this MSc.

See [referee strategy](referee-strategy.md) and [recommendation request templates](recommendation-request.md).
