# Personal statements plan

Each response must be 150-300 words. Aim for roughly 200-250 words so there is room to revise. Each answer should make a distinct point while contributing to one coherent application story.

## Core narrative

You already have strong applied experience in cloud, platform engineering, automation, software delivery, automotive systems, and technical leadership. The application should show that the MSc is a deliberate move from security-adjacent responsibility toward deeper technical cybersecurity expertise and the governance judgment needed to lead secure systems at organizational scale.

Avoid making all seven answers a CV recap. Use specific evidence, reflection, and a clear connection to the programme.

## 1. Background and why now

**Prompt:** Describe your current academic and/or professional background. Why have you decided to pursue a MSc in Cybersecurity at this stage of your studies or career?

Suggested evidence:

- Senior DevOps role spanning cloud/platform engineering, CI/CD governance, and AI automation.
- GitHub organization governance across 1,000+ repositories and 500+ developers.
- BSc in Computer Science, 9.04/10, top 1%.
- Increasing responsibility for systems where security, resilience, policy, and delivery interact.

Main point: experience has shown you the limits of treating security as an implementation detail; you now want systematic depth in security technologies, risk, data protection, and governance.

## 2. Major challenges over the next 5-10 years

**Prompt:** What are the most significant cybersecurity challenges over the next 5-10 years, and how will this MSc help you address them?

Recommended focus: AI-enabled and autonomous systems combined with software-supply-chain and cloud-platform risk. Explain that the challenge is not only new attacks, but governing fast-moving systems across technical, human, regulatory, and organizational boundaries.

Connect to courses such as Cybersecurity Governance, Privacy and Data Protection, Network Security, Offensive Security, and Secure Autonomous Systems.

## 3. Contribution to the cohort

**Prompt:** What knowledge, technical skills, professional experience, or personal perspectives would you contribute?

Suggested evidence:

- Hands-on AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Terraform, Linux, Go, Python, and CI/CD.
- Enterprise platform/governance work at large scale.
- Automotive OTA and embedded-to-cloud experience.
- Research/publication experience and startup product leadership.
- A perspective spanning implementation, operations, developer experience, business goals, and governance.

Main point: you can translate between technical depth and organizational execution and contribute grounded examples to group work.

## 4. Collaboration example

**Prompt:** Describe a collaborative technical, academic, or professional problem, your role, challenges, and lessons learned.

Best candidate example: the Sitecore DevOps Tiger Team deployment into Azure US Central under capacity constraints, completed in under a month and preventing projected SLA breaches.

Alternative: the Android Automotive port of an 80 GB embedded C++ navigation stack after previous attempts had failed.

Choose one story and cover situation, your responsibility, how the team coordinated, the hardest disagreement/constraint, measurable outcome, and what changed in your approach afterward.

## 5. Short- and long-term goals

**Prompt:** What are your short-term and long-term academic and career goals, and how will this MSc help?

Possible direction:

- Short term: lead secure platform and AI-enabled infrastructure with stronger security architecture, threat, risk, and compliance depth.
- Long term: shape cybersecurity strategy and governance as a senior technology/security leader, while contributing applied research or reusable industry practice.

Be specific about the capability gap the MSc fills; avoid relying on a job-title-only goal.

## 6. Topic of special interest

**Prompt:** Identify a cybersecurity challenge, emerging technology, innovation, or application that especially interests you.

Recommended topic: security and governance of autonomous/agentic systems, including identity, least privilege, tool access, supply-chain integrity, observability, and accountable human oversight.

This is the cleanest connection between your current AI automation work and the programme. Keep it concrete and avoid speculative claims.

## 7. Something not elsewhere in the application

**Prompt:** Share something that helps the committee understand your motivation, potential, or suitability.

Recommended angle: your unusual learning velocity and appetite for difficult, cross-disciplinary problems - completing a four-year BSc's coursework in two years as the first student in the department to do so, then moving across research, automotive, cloud platforms, enterprise engineering, and a startup.

The reflection matters more than the achievement: explain how this shaped your curiosity, discipline, humility, and willingness to learn from people with different expertise.

## Final consistency check

- Every answer directly addresses its prompt.
- At least four answers contain a concrete example.
- Claims and dates match the CV.
- The programme is discussed as both cybersecurity technologies and governance, not as a generic security degree.
- Answers do not repeat the same achievement more than twice.
- Each answer explains significance and learning, not only actions.
- Final word counts are within 150-300 words.
