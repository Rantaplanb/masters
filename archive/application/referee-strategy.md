# Referee strategy

## Recommendation

Use one strong current or recent manager and the bachelor's thesis supervisor if the supervisor remembers the work well enough to write a specific, enthusiastic recommendation.
If the supervisor would provide only a vague or hesitant letter, use two strong managers with complementary perspectives instead.

## What matters most

Recommendation quality, specificity, and direct knowledge matter more than whether the referee is academic or professional.
The programme explicitly accepts letters from university faculty and/or employers.
It does not publish a preference or extra weighting for academic referees.

The recommendation template asks the referee to address:

- How long and in what capacity they have known the candidate.
- Relevant courses, grades, and class ranking when applicable.
- Oral and written communication.
- Collaboration with others.
- Timely achievement of goals.
- Reasons the candidate fits this specific MSc.
- Whether they strongly recommend, recommend with reservations, do not recommend, or have no opinion.

## Does the referee need a cybersecurity background?

No.
A cybersecurity connection is helpful, but it is not a formal requirement.
The referee should connect directly observed evidence to qualities relevant to the programme, such as sound technical judgment, cloud and platform responsibility, secure software delivery, CI/CD governance, operational risk management, critical thinking, reliability, collaboration, and leadership.

A strong manager who knows the work in depth is preferable to a cybersecurity specialist who can provide only generic praise.

## Value of the bachelor's thesis supervisor

The thesis supervisor can add evidence that managers usually cannot provide:

- Readiness for postgraduate academic work.
- Independent research ability.
- Analytical depth and critical thinking.
- Scientific or technical writing.
- Persistence with a substantial open-ended project.
- Potential for the MSc thesis.

This would balance a professionally strong application.
However, the existing BSc result, top-one-percent ranking, publications, and professional record already demonstrate substantial academic and technical strength.
The academic letter would strengthen the application, but a weak letter would not be worth using merely for balance.

## Pairing order

1. Strong manager plus strong thesis supervisor.
2. Two strong managers who cover different evidence.
3. Strong manager plus generic academic letter.
4. Two managers who repeat the same generic praise.

## Suggested professional pairing if the academic option is weak

- A current Sitecore manager who can discuss scale, governance, platform responsibility, AI workflows, and leadership.
- A former automotive, FORTH, or other manager who can discuss a different dimension, such as deep technical problem-solving, research discipline, safety-sensitive software, or growth over time.

## Action for the thesis supervisor

Ask whether they would feel comfortable providing a strong recommendation based on the thesis or research work.
Send the current CV, transcript, thesis abstract or thesis copy, publication links, programme description, and a concise update on the career since graduation.
Use the academic referee only if the response is clearly positive and the referee remembers enough to provide concrete evidence.
