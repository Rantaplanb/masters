# Admissions process update email

- Programme: MSc in Advanced Cybersecurity Technologies and Governance
- Institution: Department of Digital Systems, University of Piraeus
- Sender: Prof. Costas Lambrinoudakis, Programme Director
- Date received: Not included in the supplied text

## Email

Dear Prospective Applicant,

Thank you for your interest in the MSc in Advanced Cybersecurity Technologies and Governance at the Department of Digital Systems - University of Piraeus. We are delighted by the overwhelming response we have received and are pleased that you are considering joining the first cohort of our new programme.

As someone who has already submitted an Expression of Interest, we would like to keep you informed about the progress of the admissions process.

We are pleased to let you know that the programme has successfully completed the academic evaluation process conducted by the Hellenic Authority for Higher Education and has received a positive assessment. Before admissions can officially begin, only one final step remains: the formal approval by the Authority's Board of Directors. This is a statutory administrative procedure required for all newly established postgraduate programmes.

We expect this final approval to be completed by the end of July. Consequently, applications are expected to open during the first week of August, subject only to the completion of this final statutory approval process.

The interest we have received so far has been exceptionally encouraging and clearly demonstrates the growing demand for high-quality postgraduate education in Cybersecurity. We are looking forward to welcoming a diverse and international cohort of talented students, and we hope that you will be among the first members of this exciting new programme.

We will make sure you are among the first to know when applications officially open. To help you prepare, we recommend that you gather the information and supporting documents that will be required during the application process:

- Personal and contact information.
- Details of your academic qualifications.
- Professional experience (if applicable).
- Evidence of English language proficiency (where applicable).
- Contact details of two academic or professional referees, who will be invited to submit a confidential recommendation.
- Your responses (personal statements) to the programme's motivation questions.

1. Describe your current academic and/or professional background. Why have you decided to pursue a MSc in Cybersecurity at this stage of your studies or career? (min. 150, max. 300 words)
2. Cybersecurity is a rapidly evolving field. What do you consider to be the most significant cybersecurity challenges over the next 5-10 years, and how will this MSc help you develop the knowledge and skills needed to address them? (min. 150, max. 300 words)
3. What knowledge, technical skills, professional experience, or personal perspectives would you contribute to your MSc cohort? (min. 150, max. 300 words)
4. Describe a situation in which you collaborated with others to solve a technical, academic, or professional problem. What was your role, what challenges did you encounter, and what did you learn from the experience? (min. 150, max. 300 words)
5. What are your short-term and long-term academic and career goals, and how will this MSc in Cybersecurity help you achieve them? (min. 150, max. 300 words)
6. Is there a cybersecurity challenge, emerging technology, innovation, or application that particularly interests you? Explain why you find it important and how you would like to contribute to this area during or after your studies. (min. 150, max. 300 words)
7. Share something about yourself that is not reflected elsewhere in your application and that you believe would help the Admissions Committee better understand your motivation, potential, or suitability for this MSc programme. (min. 150, max. 300 words)

The following supporting documents that you will be asked to upload:

- Passport or National ID card.
- Recent passport-style photograph.
- Degree certificate (or, if you are completing your studies, a Certificate of Completion of Studies or an official Transcript of Records).
- English language certificate (or equivalent supporting evidence).
- An up-to-date Curriculum Vitae (CV).

## What happens next?

- End of July: Expected completion of the final statutory approval process.
- Early August: The online application portal opens, and you will receive an email invitation with detailed application instructions.
- Following weeks: Applications will be evaluated by the Admissions Committee, and successful applicants will receive admission offers.

Our MSc has been designed to combine rigorous academic education with practical, industry-oriented learning, equipping graduates with the technical knowledge, critical thinking, and hands-on experience needed to address today's rapidly evolving cybersecurity challenges and to pursue successful careers in industry, government, and the wider digital economy.

We sincerely appreciate your patience and continued interest. We are looking forward to welcoming the first cohort of students and hope that you will be among them.

Should you have any questions in the meantime, please do not hesitate to contact us.
