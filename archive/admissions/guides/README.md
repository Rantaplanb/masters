# Admissions guides

## Applicant guide

The two-page [applicant guide](applicant-guide-uprc-2026.pdf) describes this process:

1. Select the programme and verify its requirements and deadline.
2. Create and activate the applicant account.
3. Complete all portal tasks and upload clear, valid documents.
4. Pay the application fee and wait for the payment to be recorded.
5. Review and submit the application.
6. Complete the mandatory asynchronous online video interview by the separately emailed deadline.
7. Monitor the portal and email for review, clarification requests, and the decision.
8. Complete the offer and enrolment steps applicable to the applicant's citizenship.

The application can be saved and resumed before submission.
After submission, it is locked and cannot be edited.
The application proceeds to review only after the mandatory video interview is complete.

## Study guide

The [official 2026-2027 study guide](study-guide-2026-2027.pdf) documents the programme structure, admissions criteria, recommendation template, fees, course outlines, assessment, and regulations.

Important admissions details include:

- Two recommendation letters from faculty members and/or employers are accepted.
- The study guide lists the degree certificate, academic transcript, English-language evidence, ID or passport, photograph, and CV among the core supporting records.
- A bachelor's thesis, publications, and proof of professional or research activity may also support the application when applicable.
- The dossier represents 80% of the stated evaluation, and the interview represents 20%.
- The dossier criteria are background knowledge at 30%, overall skillset at 30%, and critical thinking at 20%.
