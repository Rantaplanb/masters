# Admissions chronology

## July 2026 preparation update

The first email confirmed that the programme had received a positive academic assessment, with final statutory approval still pending.
It predicted an early-August application opening and asked prospective applicants to prepare information, documents, two referees, and seven personal statements.
It was informational and did not request a reply or submission by email.

## 11 August 2026 application opening

The second email confirmed that all formal approval procedures were complete.
It announced an application window from 13 August through Monday, 21 September 2026.
It provided the official application portal and attached the applicant guide.
