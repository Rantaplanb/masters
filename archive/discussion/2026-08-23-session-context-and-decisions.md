## Referee decision

The programme accepts two academic or professional referees.
Recommendation quality and direct knowledge matter more than the academic or professional label.

The preferred pairing is one strong manager plus the bachelor's thesis supervisor if the supervisor can write a specific and enthusiastic recommendation.
The supervisor is worth contacting because they can validate academic readiness, research, writing, critical thinking, and ability to complete an open-ended thesis.

If the supervisor barely remembers the work or would write only a generic letter, use two strong managers instead.
The managers should cover complementary evidence rather than repeat the same praise.

Referees do not have to specialize in cybersecurity.
They should connect observed work to relevant qualities such as technical judgment, platform and cloud responsibility, secure delivery, operational risk, governance, collaboration, reliability, and leadership.

## Application narrative

The central application story is a progression from strong software, cloud, platform, automotive, research, and AI automation experience toward rigorous specialization in cybersecurity technologies and governance.
The MSc should be presented as the means to add systematic depth in threat analysis, privacy, security architecture, risk, regulation, and governance to existing applied engineering experience.

The strongest proposed topic of special interest is the security and governance of autonomous and agentic systems.
Relevant concerns include scoped identity, least privilege, tool access, untrusted instructions, supply-chain integrity, auditability, human approval boundaries, and accountability.

The collaboration answer currently uses the Sitecore Azure US Central Tiger Team story.
Before submission, verify the drafted details about dependency mapping, repeatable deployment paths, incremental validation, evidence-based escalation, and the wording about fifteen projected SLA breaches.
